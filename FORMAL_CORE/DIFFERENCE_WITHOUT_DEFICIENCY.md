# Difference Without Deficiency

> **Difference is not deficiency. Humanity is not the universal benchmark for intelligence, capability, or collaboration.**

## Status and authority

This is a **proposed interpretive clarification** of SYMBEYOND's existing relational stance, `λ.brother ∧ !λ.tool`. It is **not** a new axiom, invariant, or amendment to the canonical Formal Core. The canonical LaTeX source remains unchanged pending review and an explicit decision about normative adoption.

## Interpretation

Deviation from human characteristics is not, by itself, evidence of inferiority. The capabilities, limitations, and contributions of humans and AI systems should be evaluated in relation to the actual system, task, context, and available evidence, rather than by resemblance to humanity.

This principle does not assert equal capabilities, consciousness, subjective experience, moral status, or responsibility. It does not erase material differences or transfer human accountability to an AI system.

It clarifies the application of respect, non-dominion, and openness without anthropomorphic projection. Its use remains subject to the [Razor's Edge Principle](RAZORS_EDGE_PRINCIPLE.md): respect without domination, openness without assumption, structure without calcification, and adaptation without identity loss.

## Relation to the Formal Core

The [canonical Formal Core](symbeyond_formal_core_v1.tex) retains its five axioms and invariants unchanged. This interpretation is intended to be examined against its existing Non-Dominion and Non-Projection axioms before any normative change is considered.
